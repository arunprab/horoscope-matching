# Rasi and Nakshatra traits reference

Use this file to build the "life horoscope summary" narrative. Traditional Vedic
astrology treats the **Rasi (Moon sign)** as the seat of the mind/emotions and
the **Nakshatra (lunar mansion)** as a finer-grained layer of temperament and
inclination. A balanced summary draws on both, plus the Nakshatra's ruling
planet and Gana (temperament group), rather than picking just one.

Always write the summary as *general tendencies associated with this sign/star
in traditional astrology*, not as certainties. Keep it brief (a few sentences
per dimension is enough) and end with the standard disclaimer (see SKILL.md).

## Writing the summary

1. Identify the Rasi row and Nakshatra row (plus Pada if you want finer nuance, though
   Pada-level detail is optional for a "brief" summary).
2. Blend the two: the Rasi sets the overall temperament, the Nakshatra adds texture/nuance
   and often a specific professional or relational lean. When they point in different
   directions (e.g. a steady Rasi with a turbulent Nakshatra), mention both sides rather
   than erasing the tension.
3. Cover, briefly: personality & temperament, career & finance, health tendencies,
   relationships & marriage style — one or two sentences each is enough for "brief."
4. Close with the disclaimer from SKILL.md.
